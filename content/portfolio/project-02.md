### [🌐 View Dashboard](https://ndiop.shinyapps.io/agriculture_dashboard_forecast_app/)

## Project Requirements

🗂️ **Data:** Over 216,000 FAOSTAT records covering agricultural production from 2000 to 2021.

🛠️ **Tools:** R, Shiny, Tidyverse, and Plotly.

🗺️ **Geographic Analysis:** Visualize production across countries and continents using interactive maps.

🎛️ **Interactive Filters:** Explore production by year, crop, country, and continent.

🏆 **Rankings:** Identify leading producers and top crops within selected countries.

📈 **Historical Trends:** Explore changes in agricultural production over time.

🔮 **Forecasting:** Generate linear-trend forecasts with an adjustable forecast horizon.

🚀 **Deployment:** Publish the dashboard online for interactive access.
