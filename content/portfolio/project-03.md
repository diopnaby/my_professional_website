## Project Requirements

🗂️ **Data:** Colorectal cancer patient records with survival time, event status, and patient characteristics.

🛠️ **Tools:** R, Shiny, and Plotly.

📈 **Survival Analysis:** Kaplan–Meier curves and hazard ratio visualizations.

🎛️ **Interactive Filters:** Explore patient groups by cancer stage, location, and gender.

🔮 **Predictions:** Generate survival probability estimates based on selected patient characteristics.

🚀 **Deployment:** Publish the application online for interactive access.
